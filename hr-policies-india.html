The role of policies

HR policies

Written HR policies set expectations for hiring, conduct, leave, performance and exit. They give managers a common reference and employees a clear route when questions arise.

Governance policies

Governance policies set out how the board and the company handle related party transactions, risk, whistleblower concerns, director conduct and similar areas. Which ones apply depends on the type of company.

Who it helps

  • SME founders and HR heads building a first set of policies.
  • Company secretaries, CFOs and board members of listed and public companies.
  • Companies preparing to be investor-ready.
  • HR teams streamlining policies that have grown over the years.
  • Growing teams and teams working across multiple locations.

Frequently asked questions

Why does a company need written HR policies?

Written policies set out expectations and procedures for hiring, conduct, leave, performance and exit, so that employees are treated consistently and records exist when questions arise. Some policies, such as the one required under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013, are tied to specific legal duties of the employer.

Why do companies maintain corporate governance policies?

The Companies Act, 2013 and, for listed companies, the SEBI listing regulations call for specific policies for particular classes of companies. Other companies adopt them as good practice, and investors, lenders and auditors commonly ask to see them.

What does curated mean here?

The documents are selected and organized by employee lifecycle rather than offered as a bulk collection. Each document follows a defined structure with numbered clauses, procedures and, where relevant, flowcharts.

Will the documents carry our company branding?

Yes. The documents are delivered with your company branding: your company name, logo, header and footer.

Are the templates editable?

Yes. Documents are provided as Word templates that can be edited, together with PDF versions. Templates should be customized to your facts and circumstances.

Which governance policies apply to my company?

It depends on whether the company is listed, an unlisted public company or a private company, and on size and activity. The applicability table on this site gives an indicative view. Please confirm for your facts.

Are state-specific versions available?

Where rules differ from state to state, as they can for leave and working hours, state-specific versions are available for your state.

Can individual policies be provided on their own?

The documents are grouped into HR Policies, Corporate Governance Policies and a Complete Set. If you need only certain documents, share the requirement on a call and the set can be scoped accordingly.

What is in the Complete Set?

The Complete Set brings the HR Policies and the Corporate Governance Policies together, so that employee-facing and board-level documents come from one library.

Who needs a POSH policy and Internal Committee?

Under the Act, an employer must constitute an Internal Committee at each office or branch with ten or more employees. The policy, handbook, annual report format and communication documents support the employer's duties under the Act.

What are the fillable PDF forms?

Forms such as the employee information form and policy acknowledgement can be completed on screen: type, tick and save. They are ready to complete without printing.

Do these documents help with investor due diligence?

Investors commonly ask whether employment, POSH and governance documentation is in place. A documented library gives a company a ready reference when those questions arrive.

Is this legal advice?

No. These are policy documents and templates. They are drafted with reference to applicable Indian laws, and should be customized to your facts and circumstances.
