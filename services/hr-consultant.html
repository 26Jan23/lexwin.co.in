POSH Compliance

POSH Act Compliance — From Policy to ICC to Annual Report

The Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act, 2013 is not optional. Every organisation with 10 or more employees must have a constituted Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), a compliant policy, and documented awareness training — failing which senior management and the organisation itself face personal liability.

LexWin handles the complete POSH compliance lifecycle — not just the paperwork, but the cultural groundwork that makes workplaces genuinely safer.

📝
POSH Policy Drafting
Legally compliant, gender-neutral POSH policies customised to your organisation's size, industry, and culture — in English and regional languages.
🏛️
ICC Constitution & Support
Formation of a legally valid Internal Complaints Committee with the correct composition, including an external member as required by law — and ongoing ICC member training.
🎓
Awareness & Training Programmes
Engaging, scenario-based POSH awareness sessions for employees and specialised training for ICC members on inquiry procedures and evidentiary standards.
⚖️
Complaint Inquiry & Advisory
Expert guidance to ICC during complaint inquiry — maintaining due process, protecting all parties, and producing legally defensible inquiry reports and recommendations.
📊
Annual Compliance & Reporting
Annual POSH compliance review, board reporting obligations, and the mandatory annual report submission — ensuring zero compliance gaps year on year.
₹50K
Minimum penalty per non-compliance instance under the POSH Act — with repeat violations attracting twice the penalty and cancellation of business licences.
10+
Employees is the threshold above which ICC constitution is mandatory. Remote, temporary, contractual, and gig workers all count.

1. Sexual Harassment Prevention POSH Act, 2013 ICC constitution, policy, annual training, inquiry process End-to-end POSH compliance
