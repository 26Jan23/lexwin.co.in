Data Privacy Compliance

DPDP Act & GDPR — Compliance That Goes Beyond a Privacy Policy

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is in force. Businesses that collect, store, or process personal data of Indian citizens — whether operating from India or abroad — must comply. Penalties reach ₹250 crore per breach. A privacy policy page is not compliance.

LexWin builds comprehensive data privacy frameworks — not just documents. We map your data flows, identify obligations, and implement the structures your business needs to be genuinely compliant.

🗺️
Data Mapping & Asset Inventory
Identification of what personal data you collect, where it resides, how it flows, who processes it, and what legal basis applies to each processing activity.
📜
Privacy Policy & Consent Framework
DPDP-compliant privacy notices, consent mechanisms, and data principal rights frameworks — drafted in plain language and legally precise in equal measure.
🔗
Vendor & Data Processor Contracts
Data processing agreements and vendor contracts that meet DPDP and GDPR requirements — so your third-party relationships do not become your liability.
🚨
Breach Response Planning
Incident response procedures, breach notification protocols, and the documentation required to demonstrate accountability to the Data Protection Board if a breach occurs.
👤
DPO Advisory & Board Reporting
Data Protection Officer advisory support, privacy governance structures, and board-level reporting frameworks to embed accountability at every level of your organisation.
₹250 Cr
Maximum penalty per instance under India's DPDP Act, 2023 — with separate penalties for each distinct breach of obligations.
2023
The DPDP Act received Presidential assent. Rules are being notified progressively — compliance obligations are live and enforcement is imminent.
GDPR-Regulated Businesses

If your business handles personal data of EU residents — through a website, SaaS product, or client relationship — GDPR applies regardless of where your business is incorporated. LexWin advises Indian businesses on full GDPR compliance obligations.
