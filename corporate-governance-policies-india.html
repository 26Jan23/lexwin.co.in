Corporate Governance

Corporate Governance Policies for Indian Companies

Thirteen corporate governance policy documents for Indian companies, with an applicability table for listed, unlisted public and private companies.

Corporate Governance: what is covered

Board-level and company-level policies under the Companies Act, 2013 and, for listed companies, the SEBI listing regulations. Which policies apply depends on the type and size of the company, as the table below shows.

Documents in this group

Related Party Transactions Policy
Identification, approval and disclosure of transactions with related parties.
Corporate Social Responsibility Policy
Framework for CSR activities and their monitoring.
Nomination and Remuneration Policy
Criteria for appointment and remuneration of directors, key managerial personnel and senior management.
Vigil Mechanism and Whistleblower Policy
Channel for directors and employees to report genuine concerns, with safeguards against victimization.
Code of Conduct for Directors and Independent Directors
Standards of conduct and duties for directors.
Board Evaluation Policy
Manner of evaluating the board, its committees and individual directors.
Risk Management Policy
Identification, assessment and mitigation of business risks.
Internal Financial Controls Policy
Policies and procedures for orderly conduct of business and reliable financial reporting.
Loans, Investments and Guarantees Policy
Process for inter-corporate loans, investments and guarantees.
Deposits Acceptance Policy
Framework for companies that accept deposits.
Fraud Reporting Policy
Identification and reporting of fraud.
Board Diversity and Succession Planning Policy
Diversity on the board and planning for succession.
Familiarization Programme for Independent Directors
Programme to familiarize independent directors with the company and its business.

Format

Documents are provided as editable Word templates with PDF versions, and fillable PDF forms where a form is meant to be completed on screen. They are drafted with reference to applicable Indian laws. Templates should be customized to your facts and circumstances.
